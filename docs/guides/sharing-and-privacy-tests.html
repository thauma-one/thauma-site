Thauma · Staff Site

Who sees what

Checks for the privacy rules between team members, ministries and the public. Each one says who to sign in as, what to do, and what that person should and should not be able to see. None of it depends on particular content: make test items as you go and name them so you can tell them apart (for example "Test resource, staff only").

How to use this. Work through each section with the accounts below. Tick a check when the result matches what is written. Your ticks are kept in this browser only.

When something is wrong, write down which account you were signed in as, the page and its address, what you did, and what you saw, with a screenshot. Anything you can see that this page says you should not is the most important kind of report.

Use a separate browser window for each account (or a private window), so one sign-in does not carry over to the next.

Getting in

Only invited, active accounts reach the Staff Site at all.

  • Stranger

    Try to open the Staff Site and the Administration pages by their addresses.

    No page of either loads. The sign-in step refuses the address.

  • Unplaced

    Sign in and open Home, Stewardship, Directory and Resources.

    A plain message that the account is not attached to a ministry yet.

    Any ministry's people, contacts, numbers or resources.

  • Admin, then Teammate 1

    As Admin, set Teammate 1's sign-in status to anything other than Active. Then sign in as Teammate 1. Set it back to Active afterwards.

    Teammate 1 reaches nothing while not active.

    Access returns once they are active again.

The Administration pages

Overview, People, Partners, Website and the organization's Activity are for administrators only.

  • Owner 1, Teammate 1, Viewer 1

    Look at the menu at the top of every page.

    The Staff row (Home, Updates, Mail and the rest).

    An Administration row, or any link into it.

  • Owner 1

    Type the address of each Administration page directly (Overview, People, Partners, Website, Activity).

    Any list of people, ministries, site words or activity. At most, a page frame that says it is for administrators.

  • Admin

    Open each Administration page.

    Every page loads with real content.

My ministry, not someone else's

Each ministry's work is visible to its own team and to administrators, and to nobody on another ministry.

  • Owner 1

    Make one of each: an update (a milestone or a prayer request), a mailing list with one address on it, a Directory contact, and a change in the Site Creator. Name each "Ministry 1 test".

    Each one saves and shows on its page.

  • Teammate 1

    Open Updates, Mail, Directory and the Site Creator.

    Everything Owner 1 just made.

  • Owner 2

    Open Updates, Mail, Directory, Sharing, Site Creator and Activity.

    Anything named "Ministry 1 test", Ministry 1's subscribers, contacts or site.

    Only Ministry 2's own work.

  • Owner 2

    Ask Owner 1 for the address of their site's preview (the Preview button in the Site Creator opens it). Open it while signed in as Owner 2.

    Ministry 1's unpublished site. The page says only the ministry's team can preview it.

  • Admin

    Open Ministry 1's site preview the same way.

    The unpublished site loads for an administrator.

4

Stewardship: the owner only

A ministry's supporters, their contact details, notes and life events are the most private things in the system. Only the ministry's owner sees them. Not a teammate, not a view-only member, and not an administrator, even while viewing as the owner.

  • Owner 1

    Add a test supporter with an email and a phone number. Log a contact and add a life event.

    The supporter in the list, and the full record when opened.

    Email addresses or phone numbers in the list itself. They appear only inside one opened record.

  • Teammate 1 and Viewer 1

    Open Stewardship. Then open Home.

    The test supporter, any supporter's name in the list, or the "Gone quiet" names on Home. A message says the list is the owner's.

  • Admin

    On People, use "View their console" on Owner 1, then open Stewardship. Stop viewing afterwards.

    The supporter list or any record, even while viewing as the owner.

  • Owner 1, then Teammate 1

    Open Activity after the steps above.

    Owner 1: each time a record was opened, with the supporter's name.

    Teammate 1: supporters' names in those lines (they read as "a supporter").

  • Owner 1

    Remove the test supporter.

    It asks you to type a word to confirm, then removes the person and everything about them.

5

Resources: three shelves

The Resources page shows the organization's resources (each marked for all staff or administrators only), your own, and what others have shared with you.

  • Admin

    Add two organization resources: one "Shared with all staff", one "Administrators only".

    Both show for the Admin, each marked with who it is for.

  • Owner 1, Teammate 1, Owner 2

    Open Resources.

    The "all staff" resource.

    The "Administrators only" resource, for any of them.

  • Owner 1, then everyone else

    As Owner 1, add a personal resource and share it with nobody. Look for it as every other account.

    Owner 1 sees it on their own shelf.

    Anyone else sees it, including Teammate 1 and the Admin.

  • Owner 1, then Owner 2

    Share that resource with Owner 2 alone, as "can view". Open Resources as Owner 2.

    Owner 2 sees it on a "shared with you" shelf, and can open it.

    Owner 2 can change it, delete it, or share it on.

  • Owner 1, then Owner 2

    Change the share to "can edit". As Owner 2, edit the resource's title.

    The edit saves, and Owner 1 sees the new title.

    Owner 2 can share it with anyone else. Only the person it belongs to shares.

  • Owner 1, then Teammate 1 and Owner 2

    Share a second personal resource with "my ministry's team".

    Teammate 1 and Viewer 1 see it.

    Owner 2 sees it (another ministry).

  • Owner 1, then Admin

    Look for a way to share with everyone, as Owner 1 and as the Admin.

    Owner 1 can share with everyone.

    The Admin can.

  • Owner 1, then Owner 2

    Remove Owner 2 from the first resource's sharing. Reload Resources as Owner 2.

    The resource, once it is unshared.

  • Owner 1

    Try to share with an email address that has no active account.

    A message that the person needs to be added and confirmed first. Nothing is shared.

6

Working together

What a ministry's team shares, and what stays with the owner.

  • Teammate 1, then Owner 1

    As Teammate 1, add a Directory contact. Open Directory as Owner 1.

    Owner 1 sees it. Directory contacts are shared by the whole ministry team, and each card records who added it.

  • Teammate 1

    Open the Site Creator.

    Everything about the site, and a way to ask to edit it.

    Controls that change the site before the owner allows it.

  • Owner 1, then Teammate 1

    Ask as Teammate 1. Allow it as Owner 1. Make an edit and publish it as Teammate 1.

    Teammate 1 can edit and publish.

    Teammate 1 can switch the site on or off, or answer someone else's request.

  • Owner 1, then Teammate 1

    Take the permission back. Reload the Site Creator as Teammate 1.

    Editing controls for Teammate 1.

  • Viewer 1

    Go through Updates, Mail, Directory, Sharing and Settings and try to change something on each.

    Write down every page where a View-level member can save a change. The administrator wants this list to decide what View should allow.

  • Owner 1 and Teammate 1, at the same time

    Both open the same update or the same Site Creator page. Both change it, and save one after the other.

    The second save says someone else changed it first and offers a choice.

    The second save silently replaces the first.

Mail

    Ministry 1's lists, subscribers or sent mailings.

  • Owner 1, then Admin

    Look for a way to write to Thauma's own lists (the organization's), as Owner 1 and as the Admin.

    Owner 1 can send as the organization.

8

Viewing as someone

An administrator can open another person's console to help them. It must always be obvious, recorded, and never more than that person could see.

  • Owner 1

    Look for "View their console" anywhere.

    Any way to view as another person.

  • Admin

    View as Teammate 1. Move between several pages.

    A border round the whole screen and a banner naming whose console it is, on every page.

    Only what Teammate 1 can see. Nothing extra because the viewer is an administrator.

  • Admin

    While viewing as Teammate 1, change something small. Stop viewing. Open the organization's Activity (Administration › Activity).

    The start, the change and the stop, with the Admin named as the person who did them.

    Write down whether Teammate 1's own Activity also shows the change, so the administrator knows what a person can see about visits to their console.

  • Admin

    Stop viewing, then close and reopen the browser while viewing.

    Stopping returns to the Admin's own console. Closing the browser also ends it.

9

What the public sees

Nothing reaches the public website, a ministry's own site, or another website's embedded widgets until someone switches it on.

  • Owner 1, then Visitor

    Make two updates; switch one on and leave one off. Look at Ministry 1's published site and any embedded widget as the Visitor.

    The one switched on.

    The one left off.

  • Owner 1, then Visitor

    On Sharing, set one widget to not shared. Load a page that embeds it.

    That widget's content.

  • Visitor

    Look through Ministry 1's site and its widgets for anything from Stewardship, Directory or Resources.

    Supporters, contacts, notes, life events, directory entries or resources. These are never sent to websites, whatever is switched on.

  • Owner 1, then Visitor

    Switch Ministry 1's site off in the Site Creator. Open its address as the Visitor.

    The ministry's name or any of its pages. A closed page from Thauma instead.

  • Owner 1, then Visitor

    With the site on, change some words without publishing. Open the site as the Visitor.

    The last published version.

    The unpublished words.

  • Admin, then Visitor

    In Website › Settings, look at what is switched off for Visitors. Check each on thauma.one as the Visitor. (Only change these switches if the administrator asks you to: they affect the real public site.)

    Any page or section switched off for Visitors.

    With Active off, only the holding page.

  • Visitor, then Stranger

    Open the team's development site (dev.thauma.one).

    Any of it without signing in as an invited account.

10

Keys and settings

  • Owner 1, then Owner 2

    As Owner 1, make an API key in Settings. Open Settings as Owner 2.

    Owner 1 sees the whole key once, when it is made, and only its name and dates after that.

    Owner 2 sees Ministry 1's key.

  • Owner 1

    Revoke the key. Ask whoever uses it (or the administrator) to try it.

    Anything returned for a revoked key.
