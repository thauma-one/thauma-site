Good to know

How saving works

There are three kinds of save. Knowing which one you are using is most of the learning.

1Save

Forms such as a contact or a supporter have their own button, like Save contact. Once pressed, it is stored for you and your team. Nobody outside sees it.

2Publish

On Updates and Site Creator you can change as much as you like. Nothing is public until you press Publish. Until then, Discard takes it all back.

3Send

Mail goes out only when you press Send. Save draft keeps it for later, and Send me a test sends one copy to you alone. Always send yourself a test first.

Other languages

Where something can be written in more than one language, you write in one and see another beside it. Translate fills in a first draft for you to check.

If two people edit the same thing

If someone else saves while you are working, the site tells you and lets you choose: keep yours, or take theirs. Nothing is overwritten quietly.

Please note. This is the real site: what you publish or send is seen by real people. Use names like "Test Person", send mail only to your own Test list, and remove what you add when you are done.

The test

The big three

These are what most ministries come for, so spend most of your time here. Try each one without help first. If you get stuck, note where, then carry on: getting stuck is useful to us, not a failure.

1Site Creator: your website

  • In Design, change your site's colors or type and watch the preview change.
  • In Pages, rewrite a few words on one of your pages.
  • In Navigation, change what your site's menu shows.
  • Decide: Publish it, or put everything back with Discard.

2Updates: what's happening

  • Add a milestone to the timeline, with a date.
  • Add a goal, and a prayer request.
  • Look at how they appear on a computer and on a phone.
  • Decide: Publish them, or Discard.

3Mail: telling people

  • Make a new list called Test and add your own email address. Confirm it from your inbox.
  • Write an update to the Test list, with a picture and a link. Try a different picture size, and both looks: Card and Integrated.
  • Send me a test, check it in your inbox, then Send.
  • Open it under Sent: who opened it? Send it again to yourself.

If you have time

The rest of the site

These keep a ministry organized behind the scenes. Quicker to try, and still worth a look.

Stewardship

  • Add "Test Person" and log a phone call with them.
  • Remove them again.

Directory

  • Add a contact your team would need, such as a church office.
  • Remove it again.

Resources

  • Find this guide in Resources and open it.

For advanced users: Sharing

For ministries that build their own website apart from Thauma, Sharing gives the pieces to put on it.

  • Find the sign-up form and copy its code.
  • Change how the timeline looks, between Full and Condensed.
