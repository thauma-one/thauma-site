Who we are

Servants of the Church, not its authority.

Thauma is a production ministry for the Protestant churches of Croatia and the broader Balkans. The need is simple to state: churches need help with production — audio, video, lighting, streaming, and the people who run them — and no organization exists to meet that need on the ground in this region.

These churches are small, scattered, and largely volunteer-run — and the people carrying that work often do it without much support: unfamiliar equipment, few people nearby who've solved the same problems, little chance to learn from someone who has. The need is real, and so is the isolation that comes with meeting it alone. That's what we're here to change.

Our posture

Students of the culture, never reformers of it.

We go as learners and participants of the cultures where we serve. We are not there to recreate churches in the image of any outside model — we come alongside what the local church has already established, meeting needs as those churches define them. That is why language and cultural learning come first, dominate the early years, and never fully end.

Thauma is a Missouri nonprofit in formation, serving the whole Protestant church across denominations.
